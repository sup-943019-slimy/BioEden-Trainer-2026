## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **BioEden**. The BioEden Trainer provides optional quality-of-life controls for resource management, construction, ecological restoration, animal care, and progression. It is designed for single-player use and helps players experiment with interconnected domes, power and water networks, pollution management, biomes, and planetary restoration.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a relaxed single-player experience, faster construction, easier resource management, flexible ecosystem planning, or a convenient way to explore BioEden without normal management limitations.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Resources** — Prevents selected construction and restoration resources from decreasing while building networks, recycling debris, and developing your planet.
- **Instant Construction** — Completes eligible buildings and infrastructure immediately, reducing downtime during settlement development.
- **Unlimited Power** — Keeps the available power supply stable while operating solar energy systems and connected structures.
- **Unlimited Water** — Maintains water availability for connected buildings, ecological projects, and animal habitats.
- **Pollution Control** — Freezes or reduces pollution growth to support cleaner restoration projects and easier ecosystem management.
- **Fast Progression** — Accelerates progression-tree development and unlocks eligible management improvements more quickly.
- **Animal Needs Control** — Helps maintain habitat conditions such as temperature, humidity, and vegetation for animals housed in domes.
- **DNA and Cocoon Management** — Provides convenient control over DNA samples and cocoon-related requirements when restoring extinct species.
- **Freeze Game Time** — Pauses simulation timers while you inspect layouts, plan networks, or adjust biome conditions.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

BioEden lets the Keeper wander across the planet at a relaxed pace while exploring debris sites, lakes, rivers, and restoration areas. The trainer does not replace exploration; its controls are intended to reduce resource and timing constraints while you move between projects.

### Tools & Inventory

Collect and manage materials recovered from debris sites, along with DNA samples and other resources used for construction, restoration, and animal care. Activate the relevant resource option before spending materials, then disable it when normal resource consumption is preferred.

### World & Environment

Restore the planet by recycling old structures, purifying lakes and rivers, repairing soils, and connecting domes through power and water networks. Use the trainer to test different layouts, stabilize ecological systems, accelerate construction, or control pollution while developing self-sustaining habitats.

### Combat & Weapons

BioEden is a peaceful management and restoration game without a conventional combat system. The trainer therefore focuses on construction, resources, exploration, environmental recovery, progression, and animal caretaking rather than weapons or combat abilities.
